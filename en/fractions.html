📚 Instructions - Fractions

🎯 How to Play

  1. Choose question type (only unlocked categories)
  2. Read carefully
  3. For multiple choice: click the correct answer
  4. For multi‑correct: click ALL correct options
  5. For true/false: click TRUE or FALSE
  6. For open answer: write the fraction in reduced form
  7. Press "Check" to verify
  8. After checking, press "Next"
