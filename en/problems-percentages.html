🌟 Why play problems and percentages games?

These interactive math games help children in 5th grade develop essential skills: solving everyday problems, calculating percentages, performing quick arithmetic operations, and using logical reasoning. With four different question types – Problems, Percentages, Calculations, and Logic – students build confidence and prepare for math tests in a fun, engaging way.

🎯 Skills developed: problem-solving, percentage calculation, mental math, logical thinking, and speed in arithmetic.

📚 Game Instructions – Math Problems & Percentages

🎯 How to Play

Solve problems and percentage calculations:

  1. Choose the question type (Problems, Percentages, Calculations, Logic)
  2. Read the question carefully
  3. For multiple-choice: click on the correct answer
  4. For true/false: click on TRUE or FALSE
  5. For open-ended: type the number in the box
  6. Press "Check" to verify your answer

🔢 Question Types

Problems: Everyday math problems

Percentages: Calculations with percentages (25%, 50%, etc.)

Calculations: Arithmetic operations and quick calculations

Logic: Mathematical reasoning and deductions

True/False: Statements to evaluate as true or false

Multiple Choice: 4 options, only one correct

🏆 Scoring and Lives System

Correct answer:
+2 points
Hint used:
-2 points each
Consecutive mistakes:
-1 life every 3 mistakes
5 correct answers in a row:
+1 life

⚠️ Attention: If you use too many hints, you might end up with 0 points for that question!

✨ Life Bonus: Every 5 correct answers in a row, you recover a life (maximum 3 lives).

🔄 Mistake System: You lose a life only after 3 consecutive mistakes. Each correct answer resets the mistake counter.

⏱️ Time

You have 10 minutes to answer as many questions as possible!

The timer turns red when less than 60 seconds remain.
