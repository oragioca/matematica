📚 Comma Rule:
When doing division with decimals, the comma in the quotient is placed when:
• You have finished the digits of the dividend (whole part)
• You need to "bring down a 0" to continue
• The remainder is not zero and you want to continue the division
Example: 25 ÷ 2 = 12.5
↑ The comma goes after the whole digits
