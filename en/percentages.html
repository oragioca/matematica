📚 Game Instructions - Probability

🎯 How to Play

Calculate the probability of simple events:

  1. Choose the question type (Basic Concepts, Dice, Coins, Objects)
  2. Read the question carefully
  3. For multiple-choice: click on the correct answer
  4. For true/false: click on TRUE or FALSE
  5. For open-ended: write the fraction or number
  6. Press "Check" to verify your answer

🎲 Question Types

Basic Concepts: Simple probability, certain/possible/impossible

Dice: Probability with one or two dice

Coins: Probability with one or more coins

Objects: Probability with drawing from bags, urns, etc.

True/False: Statements to evaluate as true or false

Multiple Choice: 4 options, only one correct

📊 How to calculate probability

Basic formula: Probability = Favorable outcomes / Total possible outcomes

  • Example: probability of drawing a red ball from a bag with 3 red and 5 blue = 3/8
  • Probability of getting heads when tossing a coin = 1/2
  • Probability of rolling a 4 with a die = 1/6
  • Important: Always write fractions in reduced form (e.g., 1/2 instead of 2/4 or 3/6)
