📚 How to Play – Column Division

🎯 Objective

Complete the long division by dragging digits into the correct boxes. Learn the steps of column division with whole numbers.

📝 How to Play

  1. Observe the division problem (dividend and divisor).
  2. Enter the quotient digits (top row) using the available tiles.
  3. Complete each step: multiply, subtract, bring down the next digit.
  4. In Guided Phase, colored boxes and labels help you.
  5. In Autonomous Phase, you decide where each digit goes.
  6. Click Check when you're done.

🏆 Scoring & Streak

  • Perfect solution: 100 points minus time taken.
  • Each correct streak multiplies your bonus.
  • Use Hint to see the correct order (-2 points).

🌟 Why Learn Column Division?

Column division (long division) is a fundamental math skill for elementary school. This game helps children understand place value, remainders, and the relationship between multiplication and division. Perfect for 4th and 5th graders!

Skills developed: division, multiplication, subtraction, place value, logical thinking.
