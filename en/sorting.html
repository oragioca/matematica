HOW TO PLAY:
  1. Drag the numbers from the top area to the green slots
  2. Sort the numbers from the smallest (1st) to the largest (5th)
  3. Watch out: There are decimal numbers between consecutive integers
  4. Click CHECK ORDER to verify
  5. You have 60 seconds to complete each level
  6. Use HINT if you need help (-2 points)
  7. Use RESTART to start over
TIPS FOR SORTING:
  • ● Purple = Integer number
  • ● Green = Decimal number
  • Remember: 3.2 is between 3 and 4
  • Example: 3, 3.5, 4, 4.2, 5 is the correct order
  • Compare the integer parts first, then the decimals
