Why play with fractions?

Fractions are a key concept in primary school math. This interactive game helps children understand fractions through visual examples, comparisons, and simple operations. Players learn to identify proper and improper fractions, compare fractions with the same denominator, and add simple fractions. The game includes problems that apply fractions to real-life situations, making learning fun and meaningful.

Designed for 5th grade students, this educational game builds confidence and prepares for math tests. With a scoring system, lives, and hints, it motivates kids to practice and improve. All fractions are presented in reduced form, reinforcing the concept of simplifying fractions.

📊 How to Work with Fractions

Terms: Fraction = numerator / denominator

  • Example: 3/4 means 3 parts out of 4
  • Proper fraction: numerator smaller than denominator (e.g., 2/5)
  • Improper fraction: numerator greater than or equal to denominator (e.g., 5/4)
  • Equivalent fractions: represent the same quantity (e.g., 1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6)
  • Important: Always write fractions in reduced form (lowest terms), e.g., 1/2 instead of 2/4 or 3/6
  • Rule: Divide numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor
