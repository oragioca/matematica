📘 About this game

Learn to correctly place the decimal point (comma) when performing divisions. Two progressive phases:

  • 🔵 Guided – coloured slots and step‑by‑step hints
  • 🟢 Autonomous – you decide where the comma goes

🎯 Skills you will acquire:

  • ✔ Recognising when to insert a decimal point in the quotient
  • ✔ Handling remainders by “bringing down zeros”
  • ✔ Understanding the relationship between dividend, divisor and decimal quotient
  • ✔ Building decimal numbers step by step with drag & drop
  • ✔ Strengthening mental arithmetic and place value

📚 Comma Rule:

When doing division with decimals, the comma in the quotient is placed when:
• Finished the whole part of dividend
• Need to "bring down a 0" to continue
• Remainder not zero and you want to continue
