📚 Game Instructions - Space and Figures INVALSI

🎯 How to Play

Test your geometry skills:

  1. Choose the question type (2D Shapes, 3D Shapes, Perimeter/Area, Problems)
  2. Read the question carefully and visualize the figures
  3. For multiple-choice questions: click on the correct answer
  4. For questions with multiple answers: click on ALL correct answers
  5. For true/false: click on TRUE or FALSE
  6. For open-ended answers: type the number (e.g., for perimeter)
  7. Press "Check" to verify your answer
  8. After checking, press "Next" to go to the next question

📐 Question Types

2D Shapes: Recognize figures (square, triangle, circle...), properties (sides, angles, diagonals)

3D Shapes & Properties: Cube, cuboid, spheres, vertices, edges, faces

Perimeter & Area: Calculate perimeter and area of simple figures

Geometry Problems: Applications to real-life situations (tiles, gardens, etc.)

True/False: Statements to evaluate as true or false

Multiple Choice: 4 options, only one correct

Multiple Multiple Choice: 4 options, multiple correct possible

📏 Key Concepts

  • 2D shapes: square (4 equal sides, 4 right angles), rectangle (opposite sides equal), triangle (3 sides), circle
  • 3D solids: cube (6 square faces, 8 vertices, 12 edges), cuboid, pyramid
  • Perimeter: sum of sides
  • Area: surface measurement (square: side×side; rectangle: base×height)
  • Symmetry: a figure is symmetrical if it can be divided into two equal parts by a line (axis)

The timer turns red when less than 60 seconds remain.

🎓 Educational Goal

This game helps prepare for the INVALSI math tests for the 5th grade of primary school, developing:

  • Recognition and properties of main geometric figures
  • Calculation of perimeters and areas
  • Understanding of 3D solids and their characteristics
  • Solving geometry problems from real-life situations
  • Ability to identify axes of symmetry
